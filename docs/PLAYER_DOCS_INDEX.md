## 📖 Document Overview

### [PLAYER_GUIDE.md](PLAYER_GUIDE.md)
**What:** Complete player manual  
**Length:** ~20 pages  
**When to Read:** After your first session, or before if you want deep preparation  
**Covers:**
- All 6 station roles in detail
- How to dock, fight, explore, salvage
- The universe (16 stations, 17 regions, danger zones)
- Making money and buying upgrades
- Factions and how to deal with them
- Combat tactics
- Tips for new crews
- Common mistakes and how to avoid them

**Best For:** Players who want to understand everything, reference during play

---

### [STATION_REFERENCE.md](STATION_REFERENCE.md)
**What:** Quick reference card (printable)  
**Length:** 2 pages  
**When to Read:** Print before your first session, keep on table/phone  
**Covers:**
- Each station's job in 1 paragraph
- Key controls and what to watch
- Combat checklist
- Danger zones summary
- Communication tips
- Common mistakes

**Best For:** Quick lookup during play ("Wait, what does Helm do again?")

---

### [THE_DRIFT_SETTING.md](THE_DRIFT_SETTING.md)
**What:** Universe lore and flavor (no mechanics)  
**Length:** ~15 pages  
**When to Read:** Before first session for immersion, or after if you want backstory  
**Covers:**
- The five suns and what they mean
- The Settlement War (20 years ago)
- Factions (Concord, Corsairs, Relic, Hollow) and their motivations
- Key locations (Port Vesper, the Shroud, the Maw)
- Tone and themes (space-western, scrappy underdogs)
- Mysteries (the Meridian fleet, the Shroud, the lanterns)

**Best For:** Players who care about worldbuilding, roleplayers, understanding why things are the way they are

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Which doc should I read first?
**A:** [Station Reference](STATION_REFERENCE.md) → Your station's section. Then play. Read more after.

### Q: I don't have time to read 20 pages before playing.
**A:** Good! You don't need to. Read your station's section in [Station Reference](STATION_REFERENCE.md) (5 minutes) and play. Learn by doing.

### Q: I'm a lore nerd, where's the deep stuff?
**A:** [The Drift Setting](THE_DRIFT_SETTING.md) → Full read, then [Player Guide](PLAYER_GUIDE.md) for details.

### Q: I'm a mechanics nerd, where are the numbers?
**A:** [Player Guide](PLAYER_GUIDE.md) → "Upgrades," "Danger Zones," "Making Money" sections.

### Q: Our GM said we're playing "Sandbox." What's that?
**A:** Open-play campaign, no fixed story. Read [Campaign Comparison](../scenarios/CAMPAIGN_COMPARISON.md), then [Sandbox GM Guide](../scenarios/sandbox/GM_GUIDE.md) (or ask your GM).

### Q: What's "The Long Haul"?
**A:** 5-session story campaign (Firefly-style). Read [Campaign Comparison](../scenarios/CAMPAIGN_COMPARISON.md). Your GM will brief you if you're playing it.

### Q: What's the AI director?
**A:** Optional local LLM that creates dynamic encounters and voices NPCs. Makes the universe feel alive. Not required—scenarios work fine without it. Ask your GM if it's running.

### Q: Can I read these docs in-game (on my phone)?
**A:** Yes! They're markdown files. [Station Reference](STATION_REFERENCE.md) is especially mobile-friendly.

### Q: I found a mistake/typo. Who do I tell?
**A:** Your GM, or file an issue in the repo (if it's on GitHub).
