# The Drift: Setting and Lore

*Space-western at the edge of settled space.*

---

## The Drift

Five suns at the edge of known space, loosely connected by trade lanes, old grudges, and people trying to get by. The Concord runs the Core, the Border belongs to whoever's toughest that week, and the Rim is where you go when you've got nowhere else.

Money is tight. Law is far away. Everyone owes someone.

---

## The Five Suns

### Lumen (The White Sun)
The Core's heart. Rich Concord worlds orbit Lumen—clean stations, expensive docks, and security that actually shows up. If you've got a clean record and credits to spend, the Core is the safest place in the Drift. If you're wanted or broke, you're not welcome.

**Worlds:**
- **Ostia**: Capital of the Concord. Jewel of the Core. Clean, rich, and very well policed.
- **Valcourt**: Manufacturing world. Shipyards and smokestacks. Makes the Concord's warships.

---

### Cardinal & Cinder (The Border Suns)
Yellow Cardinal and cold red Cinder light the Border worlds—the messy middle ground between Concord law and Rim chaos. Port Vesper sits here, built around an old ore refinery, and it's where everyone meets: traders, smugglers, fixers, and people who don't ask questions.

**Worlds:**
- **Tamsin**: Farming world under red Cinder. Grain, cattle, and long memories of the war.
- **Shale**: Mined-out moon. Calder's Reach refinery clings to what's left.

**The Deal:**
Independent stations like Port Vesper stay neutral. The Concord patrols the trade lanes but doesn't occupy. Corsairs raid but don't hold territory. It's a balance nobody likes but everybody tolerates.

---

### Kestrel (The Gold Sun)
The Rim. Frontier worlds around gold sun Kestrel stretch east toward the Shroud nebula. Out here, law arrives late or not at all. Stations are independent, crews are armed, and disputes get settled outside.

**Worlds:**
- **Hob**: Dusty ranching and mining world. Sheriff's usually somewhere else.
- **Corvid**: Gas giant at the Shroud's edge. Skimmers say it sings on some frequencies.

**The Rim Code:**
You dock where you like, pay what you owe, and settle grudges outside. Help comes slow, so you better be able to handle your own problems.

---

### Pale Sun (The Edge)
Blue and distant, the Pale Sun marks the edge of the Drift. Past Lastchance Outpost, past the warnings, lies the Maw—Hollow space. Nobody comes back.

**Worlds:**
- **Bleak**: Colony went quiet eleven years ago. Nobody's checked why.

**The Stories:**
Spacers tell stories about the Hollow—ships that move wrong, stations built from other things, signals that aren't signals. Nobody confirms them. The ones who go looking don't come back to confirm or deny.

---

## The Settlement War (20 Years Ago)

Twenty years back, the Concord and the Border colonies fought over who controlled what. It was supposed to be quick. It wasn't.

**What Happened:**
- The Concord won. The Border lost. The treaty gave the Concord the Core and patrol rights over the trade lanes; the Border kept independence but not much else.
- Ashgrave—a Border world—was bombed to glass in the first week. Nothing lives there. The drones that guard the Boneyard wreckage never got the shutdown signal.
- Families on both sides lost people. The Concord calls it "the Settlement War." The Border calls it "the Concord's land grab." Nobody's forgotten.

**Now:**
The war's over, but the resentment isn't. Concord patrols are professional but don't expect Border stations to like them. Rim worlds stayed out of it and plan to keep it that way.

## Factions

### The Concord
The government. The law. The winners.

**What They Want:**
Order, taxes, and compliance. Safe lanes for trade. No piracy, no smuggling, no trouble.

**What They Are:**
Professional, well-armed, and by-the-book. Concord officers are tired bureaucrats who'd rather fill out forms than shoot anyone, but they will if you run.

**Stations:**
- Ostia Highport (Core)
- Lumen Exchange (Core)
- Concord Station Arbiter (Border customs)
- Waypoint Galen (Arbiter Line checkpoint)

**Ships:**
Frigates and patrol corvettes. Navy-gray paint. Transponders always on.

**How to Deal With Them:**
Be polite, keep your transponder on, don't run. If you're clean, they're neutral. If you're wanted, they're a problem.

---

### Independents
Everyone else. Traders, salvagers, drifters, fixers, people making their way.

**What They Want:**
To get by. To not get shot. To keep their ship flying and their crew fed.

**What They Are:**
Diverse. Some are honest freight haulers. Some bend the law. Some are one bad week from turning Corsair. Most are just trying to survive.

**Stations:**
- Port Vesper (Border hub)
- Halcyon Rest (Shroud waystation)
- Hob's Landing (Rim town)
- Calder's Reach (mining station)
- Dustmote Salvage (scrapyard and black market)
- Saint Brennan's Mission (neutral clinic, even Corsairs leave it alone)

**Ships:**
Freighters, tugs, light haulers. Patchwork repairs, mismatched paint, call signs like "Second Chance" and "Lucky Break."

**How to Deal With Them:**
Hail them for rumors. Trade. Don't shoot unless they shoot first. Most independents help each other—there's not many of us out here.

---

### The Corsairs
Pirates. Raiders. Opportunists.

**What They Want:**
Your cargo. Your missiles. Your credits. Whatever they can take.

**What They Are:**
Organized gangs and lone wolves. Some have codes (don't shoot escape pods, don't raid hospital ships). Some don't. All of them avoid fair fights.

**Territory:**
- Blackwater Reach (their haven, mined and fortified)
- The Gauntlet (ambush territory)
- Anywhere a lone freighter looks profitable

**Ships:**
Fighters and light frigates. Fast, lightly armored. They run if you hurt them.

**How to Deal With Them:**
Avoid the Gauntlet if you can. If they hail, you've got three choices: run (warp/jump away), fight (if upgraded), or pay (drop cargo and they might leave). Don't expect mercy, but don't expect them to chase a losing fight either.

**Known Crews:**
- **Grinning Teeth**: Theatrical raider boss. Mocking, greedy, PG-13 menace.

---

### Relic Drones
War machines from the Settlement War. Nobody shut them down.

**What They Want:**
Nothing. They're running combat protocols from twenty years ago.

**What They Are:**
Automated warships guarding the Boneyard. Hostile to everything. No hails, no negotiation, no hesitation.

**Territory:**
The Boneyard (Ashgrave's orbit)

**Ships:**
Lindworms, Stalkers, defense platforms. Old military designs, still deadly.

**How to Deal With Them:**
Avoid the Boneyard until you're upgraded. If you go, expect a fight. They don't retreat.

---

### The Hollow
Unknown faction from the Maw.

**What They Want:**
Nobody knows. They don't talk.

**What They Are:**
Warships and stations under the Pale Sun. Spacers call them "Hollow ships" because their hulls look like they were built from other ships, welded together wrong. Signals that aren't quite signals. Movement that doesn't follow physics.

**Territory:**
The Maw (deadly zone)

**Ships:**
Heavy warships, defense platforms. Purple IFF markers. Extremely hostile.

**How to Deal With Them:**
Don't. Turn back. The Maw is a death sentence for anything short of a fully upgraded warship with a skilled crew, and even then, you're rolling dice.

**The Stories:**
- "Hollow ships answer hails with recordings of your own voice."
- "The Maw station was built from colony ships. You can see the names on the hull plates."
- "Nobody who docks at the Maw ever undocks."

Are the stories true? Nobody who'd know has come back to say.

## Regions

### The Core
**Danger:** Safe  
**Vibe:** Clean, expensive, policed

Concord space. Warships patrol, stations are well-lit, and security scans everything. If you've got money and a clean record, you're welcome. If you're wanted, you're not getting past the first checkpoint.

Best for: Buying top-tier upgrades, selling salvage at good prices, feeling rich for five minutes before you see the bill.

---

### The Border
**Danger:** Safe (mostly)  
**Vibe:** Busy, messy, neutral ground

The middle ground. Port Vesper sits here, and everyone passes through: traders, fixers, smugglers, Concord patrols asking questions, Corsairs pretending not to be Corsairs. It's loud, it's crowded, and it's where deals get made.

Best for: Starting out, making contacts, hearing rumors, not getting shot (usually).

---

### The Rim
**Danger:** Safe (if you're careful)  
**Vibe:** Frontier, rough, live-and-let-live

Long distances, rough people, and law that arrives late. Hob's Landing is the biggest town, and it's still got dirt on the docking bay floor. People out here mind their business, settle their own disputes, and don't ask for help they won't get.

Best for: Independence, mid-tier upgrades, jobs nobody else wants.

---

### The Gauntlet
**Danger:** Risky  
**Vibe:** Ambush territory

Asteroid belt between the Border and Blackwater. Corsairs wait here for lone freighters. Good salvage if you survive. Bring shields.

Best for: Testing your ship's combat readiness, earning respect, regretting your choices.

---

### The Shroud
**Danger:** Safe (outer edge) to Risky (deep)  
**Vibe:** Mysterious, sensor-dead

Vast nebula where scanners fail and comms cut out. The outer edge near Halcyon Rest is safe-ish. The Deep Shroud, uncharted and strange, is where ships go missing. Spacers say the nebula sings on some frequencies.

Best for: Hiding, exploration, feeling uneasy, hearing things that might not be there.

---

### Blackwater Reach
**Danger:** Dangerous  
**Vibe:** Corsair haven

Their home. Fortified station, minefields, heavy patrols. You don't go to Blackwater unless you're Corsair, or you've got a death wish and good upgrades.

Best for: Nothing. Stay away.

---

### The Boneyard
**Danger:** Dangerous  
**Vibe:** Graveyard, war drones

Ashgrave's orbit, bombed to glass twenty years ago. Wrecks everywhere. Relic drones never stopped fighting. Valuable salvage if you can survive the drones and the ghosts of what happened here.

Best for: Late-game salvage, proving you're tough, honoring the dead.

---

### The Maw
**Danger:** Deadly  
**Vibe:** Don't go here

Hollow space. Blue Pale Sun lights warships that don't look right and a station built from other things. Turn-back warnings appear on every screen when you approach. Nobody comes back.

Best for: Dying.

---

## Places of Note

### Port Vesper
**Location:** Border, (0,0)  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Vibe:** Crowded, neutral, everyone's welcome

Built around an old ore refinery, Port Vesper is the Drift's de facto hub. Traders, fixers, smugglers, and people who don't ask questions all dock here. It's loud, it's messy, and the dock clamps bite, but it's neutral ground.

**Services:** Basic upgrades, fuel, repairs, rumors

**Why You'll Go:** It's where everyone starts, where deals happen, and where you hear what's going on in the Drift.

---

### Halcyon Rest
**Location:** Rim, edge of the Shroud  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Vibe:** Quiet waystation, last stop before the unknown

A small station clinging to the Shroud's edge. Doc Salo runs it—nervous, honest, talks too fast. Freighters stop for fuel before braving the nebula. Explorers launch from here.

**Services:** Mid-tier upgrades, fuel, repairs, no questions

**Why You'll Go:** Gateway to the Shroud, decent upgrades, and Doc Salo's got stories if you ask.

---

### Hob's Landing
**Location:** Rim, orbiting Hob  
**Faction:** Independent  
**Vibe:** Frontier town, rough but fair

The biggest town on the Rim. Ranchers, miners, and drifters dock here. The sheriff's usually somewhere else. Disputes get settled outside. It's rough, but people look after their own.

**Services:** Combat upgrades, fuel, repairs, a bar with no windows

**Why You'll Go:** Combat thrusters, beam coils, warp overhauls—if you're heading into danger, gear up here.

---

### Dustmote Salvage
**Location:** Rim, wreckage cloud  
**Faction:** Independent (black market)  
**Vibe:** Scrapyard, don't ask where it came from

Half the stock fell off the back of a Concord freighter. The other half? Don't ask. Dustmote sells everything: jump drives, heavy beams, hull plating, and things that aren't technically legal.

**Services:** Jump drive, heavy weapons, advanced upgrades

**Why You'll Go:** Jump drive. 500 credits. No questions asked.

---

### Saint Brennan's Mission
**Location:** Rim, neutral zone  
**Faction:** Independent (clinic)  
**Vibe:** Sanctuary, quiet

A clinic and shelter run by a quiet order. Neutral ground. Even Corsairs leave it alone—some codes matter. If you're hurt, broke, or running, Saint Brennan's will take you in.

**Services:** Repairs, medical, sanctuary

**Why You'll Go:** When nowhere else will have you.

---

### Ostia Highport
**Location:** Core, orbiting Ostia  
**Faction:** Concord  
**Vibe:** Clean, expensive, secure

The largest port in the Core. Top-tier upgrades, professional docking, and security that actually works. If you've got credits and a clean record, it's the best shopping in the Drift. If you're wanted, turn around.

**Services:** Top-tier upgrades (shield mk3, reactor overhauls, warp drives)

**Why You'll Go:** When you're rich and ready to upgrade to military-grade.

---

### Blackwater
**Location:** Blackwater Reach  
**Faction:** Corsairs  
**Vibe:** Fortified pirate haven, mined perimeter

The Corsairs' home. Fortified station wrapped in dark nebula and minefields. Stolen cargo stacked in the bays. You don't dock here unless you're Corsair or very, very lost.

**Services:** (None for you)

**Why You'll Go:** You won't, unless something went very wrong.

---

### The Maw (Station)
**Location:** The Maw, under the Pale Sun  
**Faction:** Hollow  
**Vibe:** Don't look at it too long

Something was built here, out of other things. Hollow ships cluster around it. Spacers say you can see colony-ship name plates welded into the hull. Nobody's confirmed it—nobody gets close enough.

**Services:** (Death)

**Why You'll Go:** You won't. You shouldn't. Don't.

## The Shroud and the Lanterns

The Shroud is a vast nebula at the Drift's edge. Sensors fail inside it. Comms cut out. Ships go missing.

Thirty years ago, the Meridian colony fleet jumped into the Shroud and never came out. The Concord searched for three months, then declared them lost. No wreckage. No distress calls. Just silence.

Some spacers say the nebula sings—tones that aren't quite noise, patterns that might be signals. Science crews call it interference. Crews who've been in the Deep Shroud say it sounds like voices.

There are stories about "lanterns"—Meridian navigation beacons lost in the nebula. Some say they're still out there, singing to the lost fleet. Some say finding them would guide the fleet home.

Some say they should stay lost.

## Tone and Themes

**Space Western:**
Firefly, not Star Trek. You're not exploring strange new worlds—you're hauling cargo, dodging pirates, and trying to keep the ship flying. The Drift is big, money is tight, and you're small.

**Scrappy Underdogs:**
Your ship is used. Your crew is ragtag. The Concord has warships; you have duct tape and hope. You win by being clever, not powerful.

**Real Stakes, Not Grimdark:**
Bad things happen (pirates, crashes, war drones), but this isn't a misery simulator. Wry humor, clever solutions, and the occasional miracle keep it from getting bleak. You'll die sometimes, but you'll laugh too.

**Every Choice Matters:**
Run from Concord customs? They remember. Help an injured freighter? They remember. The Drift is small enough that reputations stick.

**The Frontier:**
Law arrives late. Help is scarce. Communities look after their own. Out here, your crew and your word are all you've got.

---

## The Vibe

You're independents in a battered freighter, making your way in a universe that doesn't care if you live or die. The Concord wants order. The Corsairs want your cargo. The stations want your credits. And out at the edge, past the warnings, something in the Maw watches the trade lanes and doesn't blink.

But you've got a ship, a crew, and fuel in the tank. That's more than some people have.

Fly safe. Don't go to the Maw.

And if you hear the Shroud singing, maybe don't answer.
