# Station Quick Reference Card

Print this or keep it on your phone during play.

---

## 🎮 HELM

**Job:** Steer the ship, don't crash

**Controls:**
- **Impulse**: Slow local travel
- **Warp**: Fast travel (drains energy, can't use in combat)
- **Jump**: Instant teleport (if upgraded, 90s cooldown)
- **Docking**: Slow down (<100 speed) → Request Dock → Follow path

**Watch:**
- Asteroids (dodge or slow down)
- Nebulae (sensors reduced)
- Mines (avoid!)
- Enemy range (get beams close, keep missiles far)

**Combat:** Turn to aim beams, keep moving to spread damage

**Docking:** Under 100 speed, request dock, follow the funnel

---

## 🎯 WEAPONS

**Job:** Shoot things before they shoot you

**Systems:**
- **Beams**: Auto-fire in arc. You aim (with Helm), manage power.
- **Missiles**: Load → Target → Fire. Limited ammo.

**Missile Types:**
- Homing: Fire and forget (most common)
- Nuke: Big boom (rare, save for big threats)
- EMP: Disables systems
- Mine: Drops behind you (defensive)

**Watch:**
- Missile count (restock at stations)
- Beam heat (red = offline, let cool)
- Shield status (down = taking hull damage)

**Tips:**
- Load tubes before fights, not during
- Target weak enemies first (scouts, fighters)
- Call out incoming missiles to Helm

---

## ⚙️ ENGINEERING

**Job:** Keep everything running when it's on fire

**Manage:**
- **Power**: 0-300% per system (more = better but hotter)
- **Coolant**: Drag from pool to hot systems
- **Repair**: Send crews to broken systems (takes time)

**Systems:**
- Reactor (power source)
- Shields (front/rear, regenerate with power)
- Engines (impulse, warp, jump)
- Weapons (beams, missiles)
- Scanners (Science needs these)

**Watch:**
- Heat: Orange = hot, Red = overheating, Flashing = offline
- Damage: Yellow = damaged, Red = offline
- Power pool: Don't overdraw (reactor can't keep up)

**Combat:**
- Boost shields 200-300%
- Cut power to non-essentials (warp, jump)
- Pre-cool weapons before fights (max power + coolant)
- Repair critical systems first (reactor, shields, engines)

## 🔬 SCIENCE

**Job:** Know what's out there before it kills you

**Controls:**
- **Scan**: Click object → Hold button. Reveals details.
- **Probes**: Launch to scan distant objects (limited supply)

**What You Learn:**
- Ships: Faction, type, shields, hull, threat level
- Stations: Faction, services, friendliness
- Salvage: Value, contents (credits, missiles, gear)
- Anomalies: Black holes, wormholes, derelict logs

**Scan Depth:**
- Simple: Quick, basic info
- Full: Slow, complete details + hidden cargo/logs

**Tips:**
- Scan everything—might be treasure or a warship
- Fully scan derelicts (reveals extra salvage + story)
- Call out threats: "Frigate, 12 o'clock, 15u, Corsair, shields up!"
- In combat: scan targets, report weakest enemy

---

## 📡 RELAY

**Job:** Big picture, comms, navigation

**Screens:**
- **Sector Map**: All contacts, stations, waypoints
- **Database**: Ship types, factions, lore
- **Objectives**: Mission goals
- **Comms Log**: Incoming messages

**What You Do:**
- Set waypoints for Helm (click map)
- Track enemy positions, warn of ambushes
- Read hails, relay to Captain
- Check reputation (credits displayed here)
- Hail stations for rumours/services

**Tips:**
- Zoom out for sector view, zoom in for local detail
- Learn faction colors (blue = Concord, red = Corsairs, etc.)
- Waypoints help Helm navigate complex routes
- Hails often have choices—Captain decides, you click

## 🌌 UNIVERSE QUICK REF

### Danger Zones
- **Safe** (Green): Core, Border, Rim, Shroud edge
- **Risky** (Yellow): Gauntlet, Deep Shroud
- **Dangerous** (Orange): Blackwater, Boneyard
- **Deadly** (Red): The Maw (avoid!)

### Factions
- **Independent** (Gray): You, traders, most stations
- **Concord** (Blue): The law. Neutral unless you attack/flee.
- **Corsairs** (Red): Pirates. Always hostile.
- **Relic** (Yellow): War drones. Hostile to all.
- **Hollow** (Purple): Unknown. Extremely hostile.

### Key Stations
- **Port Vesper** (0,0): Starting point, basic upgrades
- **Halcyon Rest** (32u east): Mid-tier upgrades, Shroud edge
- **Hob's Landing** (66u east): Combat upgrades
- **Dustmote Salvage** (80u east): Jump drive, heavy weapons
- **Ostia Highport** (Core): Top-tier upgrades (expensive, no wanted ships)

## 💰 MAKING MONEY

1. **Salvage**: Scan debris, collect pods (15-200cr each)
2. **Missions**: Campaign jobs pay 200-600cr
3. **Survey**: First visit to region = small bonus
4. **Trade**: Follow rumors (if offered)

**Credits = Reputation Points** (shown on Relay)

## 🛠️ UPGRADE PRIORITIES

**First 500cr:**
1. Hull Plating (150cr) — More HP
2. Shield MK2 (200cr) — Better shields
3. Repair Crew (150cr) — Faster repairs

**Next 500-1000cr:**
4. Jump Drive (500cr) — Escape tool
5. Forward/Heavy Beams (300cr) — More damage
6. Combat Thrusters (300cr) — Better turning

**Late Game:**
7. Hull Plating 2 (400cr)
8. Shield MK3 (400cr)
9. Warp Overhaul (400cr)
10. Reactor Overhaul (500cr)

## ⚔️ COMBAT CHECKLIST

**Before:**
- [ ] Science: Scan enemies
- [ ] Engineering: Boost shields, cool weapons
- [ ] Weapons: Load missiles
- [ ] Helm: Position for beam arcs

**During:**
- [ ] Helm: Keep beams on target, evade missiles
- [ ] Weapons: Fire missiles, manage heat
- [ ] Engineering: Balance power, route coolant, repair damage
- [ ] Science: Scan threats, call weakest target
- [ ] Relay: Track enemies, warn of reinforcements

**After:**
- [ ] Helm: Collect salvage
- [ ] Engineering: Repair damage
- [ ] Relay: Check for more hostiles

**Fleeing:**
- Helm: Warp or jump away
- Engineering: Max engines, rear shields
- Weapons: Drop mines (if equipped)

## 🆘 COMMON MISTAKES

- ❌ Docking too fast (crash!)
- ❌ Not scanning (miss danger/treasure)
- ❌ Overdrawing power (systems shut down)
- ❌ Fighting with shields down (expensive!)
- ❌ Warping into asteroids (collision!)
- ❌ Visiting the Maw early (death!)
