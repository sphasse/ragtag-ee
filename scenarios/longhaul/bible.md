## The Long Haul — campaign bible (story only)

The universe itself (regions, stations, factions, traffic) is described to the director separately, generated from `ee/ragtag/verse/`. This bible holds only the Long Haul story.

### Recurring cast
- Mags Oduya, fixer at Port Vesper. Dry, warm, quietly dangerous. Calls the crew "my lambs". Always owed a favour.
- Lt. Corin Vashti, Concord officer on CPV Diligence. Tired, by-the-book, decent underneath.
- Doc Salo, dockmaster and medic at Halcyon Rest. Nervous, honest, talks too fast.
- Grinning Teeth, Corsair raider boss. Mocking, theatrical, PG-13 menace.
- Admiral Hesper Vane (introduced session 3): Concord flag officer. Calm, polite, ruthless.
- Rook (introduced session 2): rival salvager captaining the Second Chance with her sons. Competitive, not evil.

### The five-session arc
1. Cold Cargo — A delivery job for Mags. One crate is light and humming. A derelict in the Shroud, Wren's Folly, hides a sealed data core with a lantern sigil.
2. Salvage Rights — The crew go deeper into the Shroud for "the rest of the shipment". Rook's crew races them for salvage. A Ghost ship answers the core's hum with tones Science can decode.
3. The Arbiter Line — To reach the far side of the Shroud, the Magpie must slip a Concord blockade. Admiral Vane appears and wants the lanterns very badly.
4. Old Debts — Someone sells the crew out (Mags, unless the crew trusted Doc Salo instead). Grinning Teeth take a hostage. Rescue, heist and running fight.
5. Last Light — In the heart of the Shroud the crew can light the lantern array. Vane's task force arrives. The Ghosts arrive. Old allies (or enemies) show up depending on earlier choices.

### Secrets (reveal gradually; never before the session noted)
- The lanterns are navigation beacons of the Meridian colony fleet, lost in the Shroud thirty years ago. (Hint from session 2; reveal in session 4.)
- The Ghosts are Meridian's descendants, still out there, kept alive by old machines. The lanterns are their way home. (Hint in sessions 2-3; reveal in session 5.)
- The Concord abandoned Meridian to cover up a failed evacuation; Vane signed the order as a young officer. (Reveal in session 4 or 5.)
- Mags knows more than she says and plans to sell the lanterns to the highest bidder. (Hint from session 1; confirm in session 4.)
