# Sandbox Campaign

Open-play scenario for learning the universe and ship systems. No fixed story—the crew explores, takes jobs, and makes their own narrative. Perfect for:
- First-time crews learning Empty Epsilon
- Groups between story campaigns
- Testing ship upgrades and combat
- GM experimentation with the AI director

## Structure
- Start with a modest ship and some credits
- Explore the Drift universe (stations, trade lanes, danger zones)
- Encounter opportunities: salvage, trade, distress calls, odd jobs
- Earn credits, buy upgrades, build reputation
- Sessions end when the crew docks at a friendly station and everyone's satisfied

## Features
- All universe locations accessible (but deadly zones require upgrades)
- AI director creates dynamic encounters based on location and crew choices
- Persistent credits and upgrades across sessions
- No scripted objectives—crew decides what to do
- Works great with or without the AI director

## GM Tools
- Spawn salvage, enemies, or opportunities on demand
- Give/take credits for custom situations
- Adjust danger level mid-session
- Full director control (instructions, standing directives, action approval)

## Session Length
Flexible: 30 minutes to 3 hours. End whenever the crew returns to port and the group is done.
