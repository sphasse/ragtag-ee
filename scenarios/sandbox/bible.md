## Sandbox Campaign — bible

This campaign has no fixed story. The crew explores the Drift, learns the setting, and encounters opportunities as they arise. The AI director creates dynamic situations based on crew choices and location.

### Tone
Space-western sandbox. Wry humour, real stakes, never grimdark. The crew are independents making their way on the frontier. Every action has consequences, but nothing is scripted.

### Recurring cast (may appear based on location and director choices)
- Port Vesper Dockmaster: neutral, professional, knows everyone's business
- Calder's Reach Foreman: gruff miner, suspicious of outsiders
- Hob's Landing Sheriff: tired lawman, slow to act but doesn't forget
- Various freighter captains: sources of rumours and trade gossip
- Corsair raiders: opportunistic pirates, avoid fair fights
- Concord patrols: by-the-book, can be reasoned with if you're respectful

### The universe
The Drift spans five suns from the rich Concord Core to the deadly Maw. Money is tight, law is far away, everyone owes someone. The Settlement War ended twenty years ago with the Concord in charge; Border and Rim worlds remember losing.

**Regions:**
- **The Core** (Lumen, white sun): Rich Concord worlds. Safe, expensive, heavily policed.
- **The Border** (Port Vesper, red sun Cinder): Independent trading posts. Busy lanes, loose law.
- **The Rim** (Kestrel, gold sun): Frontier worlds. Long distances, rough folk, opportunities.
- **The Shroud**: Vast nebula. Sensors fail, ships go missing, spacers tell stories.
- **The Gauntlet**: Asteroid belt. Corsair ambush territory. Risky salvage.
- **Blackwater Reach**: Corsair haven. Mined and fortified.
- **The Boneyard**: Settlement War battlefield around dead Ashgrave. Relic war drones still fight.
- **The Maw** (Pale Sun, blue): Hollow space. Deadly. Nobody comes back.

### Opportunities the director may introduce
- Salvage: debris fields with pickings (scan to reveal, but claim beacons mean someone owns it)
- Trade rumours: profitable cargo routes, shortages at specific stations
- Distress calls: ships under attack, malfunctioning stations, ambush bait
- Odd jobs: escort a freighter, deliver urgent cargo, recover stolen goods
- Encounters: other independents (friendly, rival, or hostile), patrols, pirates
- Mysteries: strange signals, derelicts with logs, things that don't belong

### Factions
- **Independent**: The crew and most traders. Neutral to each other.
- **Concord**: The law. Neutral unless provoked; hostile if crew is wanted.
- **Corsairs**: Pirates. Hostile to traders, avoid strong targets.
- **Relic**: War drones from the Settlement War. Hostile to all.
- **Hollow**: Unknown faction from the Maw. Hostile, mysterious.
- **Derelict**: Dead ships, salvageable.

### Rules for sandbox play
- Credits carry between sessions via campaign save
- Upgrades persist; the ship gets better over time
- Reputation matters: attacking neutrals, ignoring distress calls, or running from the law has consequences
- The director remembers: places visited, people helped or betrayed, enemies made
- No fixed ending: sessions end when the crew docks at a friendly station and the GM calls it
